Formats

The files of your simulations, as they are.

Open the trajectory with its topology and SciVelyx MD finds the rest: type labels and bonds from a LAMMPS data file, residues and charges from a tpr or psf, energies from the log. Files are read frame by frame, so a run of millions of atoms opens in seconds.

01

LAMMPS

Text and binary dumps with any columns, data files with eight atom styles, logs, and the ReaxFF bond and species files.

02

GROMACS, NAMD, AMBER

tpr, gro, xtc, trr and energies; psf, dcd, coor and NAMD logs; prmtop and NetCDF; HOOMD-blue GSD and MOL2.

03

DFT structures

Every ionic step of Quantum ESPRESSO, CASTEP and FHI-aims with its energy; VASP POSCAR and XDATCAR; Gaussian cube files with their grid.

Materials

Crystals, liquids and defects — checked against LAMMPS and OVITO.

Each analysis follows the definition of the LAMMPS compute or OVITO modifier it replaces, and the tests compare it with them atom by atom on real runs.

01

Structure

CNA and adaptive CNA, polyhedral template matching with orientations, Ackland–Jones, diamond, Chill+ for ice, VoroTop, Steinhardt order parameters, centrosymmetry.

02

Deformation and defects

Displacements, atomic and elastic strain, Wigner–Seitz vacancies and interstitials, grain segmentation, dislocation lines and Burgers vectors (DXA).

03

Dynamics and distributions

RDF, MSD and diffusion with its error, VACF, bond angles, density profiles, Voronoi cells, clusters, spatial correlation, histograms over time.

Biomolecules

From the RMSD to the salt bridges.

The analyses of VMD and of the GROMACS tools in one menu, with proteins made whole across the periodic boundary and every result ready for SciVelyx Graphs.

  • Structure and dynamics. RMSD after fitting, RMSF per atom and residue, radius of gyration, principal components.
  • Interactions. Hydrogen bonds with their occupancy, salt bridges, residue contact maps.
  • Per residue, in time. DSSP secondary structure, SASA and timelines of any per-residue quantity as heat maps.
  • Checked. Against MDAnalysis and MDTraj on the official test files.

Pipeline and representations

Modify as in OVITO, draw as in VMD.

54 modifiers apply to every frame you see, analyse, film or export: select, delete, replicate, slice, transform, colour, label — and draw licorice, cartoons, surfaces, density maps and polyhedra, several of them in one picture.

Scripts

Python, in the window and on the cluster.

A Python console and script editor drive the window; the same commands run in scripts and in the terminal, without a window.

  • Every reader and analysis as a command: load, rdf, msd, ptm, rmsd, salt_bridges …
  • The pipeline in code: modify(t, [Replicate(nx=2), Slice(distance=5)]).
  • Pictures and movies without a window, and send() to SciVelyx Graphs.
  • Command line: scivelyx-md rdf, msd, modify, image, script … for batch jobs.

SciVelyx Graphs

From the trajectory to the figure.

Ctrl+G opens any result in SciVelyx Graphs: sheets with names and units, graphs in which every element is editable, fits, and export at the exact size of the journal. Each sheet remembers the file, the frames and the parameters it came from.

FAQ

Questions, answered plainly.

More in the FAQ of the manual.

Which simulation programs does SciVelyx MD read?

LAMMPS (with ReaxFF), GROMACS, CHARMM and NAMD, AMBER and HOOMD-blue trajectories, and the structures of Quantum ESPRESSO, CASTEP, FHI-aims and VASP. The formats page lists every file.

Does it replace OVITO and VMD?

For analysis and figures, mostly: it covers 99.4 % of the list of OVITO 3 Basic and 100 % of the list of VMD features kept by the project, and the tests compare it with OVITO, LAMMPS, MDAnalysis and MDTraj. It does not read their session files.

Does it run simulations?

No. It reads, analyses and draws the output of the simulation programs.

How large can a system be?

Frames are read on demand: four million atoms open in about a second from a LAMMPS binary dump, with about 1 GB of memory. See Large systems.

Which language is the interface in?

English. The user manual and this site are in English and Portuguese.
